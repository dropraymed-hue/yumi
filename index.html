How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Yumi › Cancel Subscription. In Yumi: Profile › Settings › Subscription › Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I paid but Premium isn’t on

Open Yumi › Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you paid with.

How do I delete my account?

Yumi › Profile › Settings › Account › Delete account. This deletes your account, profile photo and favorites from our server. It doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that as shown above.

Is my photo stored?

No. It is sent once, with your permission, to identify the ingredients and is not kept by Yumi. Details in the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn off the AI?

Yumi › Profile › Settings › Privacy and AI › Turn off. Scanning and live recipes stop; the sample fridge, built-in recipes and your favorites keep working.

A recipe looks wrong

Open it and tap “Report this recipe” at the bottom. Recipes from the AI can contain mistakes: always check allergens.

Are the calories exact?

They are AI estimates per serving, useful for planning but not medical advice.
