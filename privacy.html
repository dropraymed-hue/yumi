Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to the Yumi app for iPhone and this website.

This policy explains what Yumi collects, why, and the choices you have. Yumi has no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools, and we never sell your data.

Who is responsible

The data controller is the developer named on the App Store, address on the App Store listing. Contact for any privacy question, including our data protection contact: see the App Store listing.

What Yumi uses and why

  • Your OK first. Before anything is sent to the AI, Yumi asks for your permission. You can withdraw it at any time in Profile › Settings › Privacy and AI; scanning and live recipes then stop, the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Photos you choose to scan. When you take or pick a photo, Yumi sends that photo to our server and on to Anthropic’s Claude AI, which lists the ingredients it sees. We don’t store the photo. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models by default, and keeps it only for a limited time as described in its privacy policy.
  • Ingredients and preferences. To suggest recipes and translate the app, Yumi sends your ingredient list, chosen cuisine, training goal, diet and allergies (if you set them), the dishes already shown and the app language to Claude in the same way. Your diet and allergies are kept on your iPhone and only sent with a recipe request; we don’t store them. Your training goal and calorie targets stay on your iPhone: they are not stored on our server.
  • AI content is labelled. Recipes, nutrition values and translations made by the AI are marked as AI-generated in the app, and each AI recipe has a “Report this recipe” link.
  • A random user ID. On first launch Yumi creates an anonymous account (a random identifier, no name or email) so only the app can use our server, and so we can count how many scans and recipe requests each person makes per day to prevent abuse.
  • Your account, if you create one. With Sign in with Apple, Google or email: your email address (with Apple this can be a private relay address), the name you give or that Apple or Google shares, no password (with email, each sign-in uses a one-time code we email you), your favorite cuisine and saved favorites, so they follow you to other devices. With Google, Yumi also receives your Google profile picture link.
  • Phone number and profile photo, if you add them. Both are optional. The phone number is kept with your account. The profile photo is reduced to a small square and stored in private storage that only your account can open.
  • The community, if you use it. Your profile is public to other Yumi users: your username, name, profile photo, bio, the photos you post with their Yumi recipe and caption, how many people you follow and who follows you. Your likes and comments are shown too. All of this is stored on our server. When you report a post or comment or block someone, we keep that report or block so we can review it and hide what you blocked. We use reports only to moderate the community.
  • Weight, height, age and sex, if you add them. Optional. They are used only on your iPhone to work out your daily calories and protein, and are never sent to our server or to the AI.
  • What you ate. The meals and snacks you add to “Your day” stay on your iPhone (the last 35 days) and are never sent anywhere.
  • Subscriptions. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card details. RevenueCat, our subscription service, receives your purchase receipts and your Yumi user ID to know whether your subscription is active.
  • On your iPhone only. Your favorites (without an account), settings, language and your photo-analysis choice stay on your device.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: accounts, database and server (hosting).
  • Anthropic: Claude AI, for photo analysis, recipes and translations.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store payments.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, if you choose it.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

They only process data to provide these services to Yumi.

Why we may use it (legal bases)

  • Your consent: sending photos, ingredients and preferences to the AI. For allergies, which are health information, this is your explicit consent (GDPR article 9(2)(a)). Withdraw it any time in Profile › Settings › Privacy and AI.
  • Contract: your account, favorites and subscription.
  • Legitimate interest: the random user ID and daily usage counts that protect the service from abuse, and reviewing reports to keep the community safe.
  • Legal obligation: what we must keep or disclose by law.

Transfers outside your country

Anthropic and RevenueCat are in the United States, and our providers may process data in other countries. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework used by these providers. For people in Algeria, data is processed abroad on the basis of your explicit consent and under Law 18-07 on the protection of personal data, as amended; Yumi’s processing is declared to the ANPDP.

How long we keep it

Account data, saved favorites, phone number, profile photo, posts, likes and comments are kept until you delete them or your account; a post you delete is removed at once. Reports are kept for up to 12 months after review. Daily usage counts are kept for up to 30 days. Photos are not stored by Yumi.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account any time in Yumi: Profile › Settings › Account › Delete account. This deletes your account, profile, phone number, profile photo, favorites, posts with their photos, likes and comments from our server, revokes Sign in with Apple, and erases your body data from the iPhone. A subscription is billed by Apple: cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
  • Use Yumi without an account, or type your ingredients instead of scanning a photo.
  • You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, to limit or object to its use, or withdraw your consent, by writing to the contact address above. We answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the CNIL in France or the ANPDP in Algeria.

Children

Yumi is not directed to children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Health

Calories, protein and other nutrition values are AI estimates for information only. They are not medical or dietary advice.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top.
