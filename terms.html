The service

Yumi lists ingredients from a photo or from what you type, and suggests recipes from many countries with estimated nutrition values. Some features use artificial intelligence (Anthropic’s Claude) and need an internet connection.

AI suggestions

Ingredient lists, recipes, translations, calories and protein are generated automatically, labelled as AI-generated in the app, and can be wrong. Use “Report this recipe” in a recipe to tell us about a wrong or inappropriate one. Check ingredients for allergies and dietary needs, cook meat, fish and eggs safely, and don’t rely on Yumi for medical or dietary advice.

Yumi Premium

  • New subscribers may get a free trial. When it ends, the subscription starts and you are charged unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel them in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Fair use
